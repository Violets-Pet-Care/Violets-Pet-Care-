Meet Violet

Experienced hands. A gentle heart. Care that feels personal.

Animals have been an important part of my life and work for years. With 16 years of hands-on grooming experience, I’ve learned that every pet has their own personality, comfort level, and needs—and that patience and kindness make a real difference.

Violet’s Pet Care grew from that same belief: your pets are family. Whether I’m stopping in to refresh water, follow a familiar routine, give medication as instructed, or send you a photo while you’re away, my goal is to help you feel confident that your pets are being cared for thoughtfully.

My professional grooming background also helps me notice changes in coat condition, comfort, or hygiene that may be worth sharing with you. For pets with more complex grooming needs, I can discuss whether a veterinary-coordinated approach is appropriate.

  • Individualized attention and familiar routines
  • Photo updates and clear communication
  • Respect for each pet’s comfort, safety, and boundaries
  • Serving Hickory, Granite Falls, and Hudson, North Carolina
