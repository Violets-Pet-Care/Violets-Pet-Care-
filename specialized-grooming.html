Specialized service option

Specialized Grooming Inquiry

Need a sedated dog shaved or help with a pet who cannot tolerate a conventional grooming salon? Contact Violet to discuss whether a veterinary-coordinated grooming visit may be appropriate.

Who this may help

Pets with severe matting or pelted coats, hygiene needs, fear or handling challenges, senior pets, or complex care needs.

How pricing works

There is no single flat price. A quote is provided after discussing the pet’s size, coat condition, time required, handling needs, and any coordination with the veterinary team. Please ask for a quote before scheduling.

Veterinary coordination

When needed, care is coordinated with your veterinary team. Sedation and medical decisions are handled only by the veterinarian and veterinary team.

Violet has 16 years of hands-on grooming experience.
